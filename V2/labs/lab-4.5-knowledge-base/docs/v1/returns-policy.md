# Returns policy

Trailhead Gear is a fictional outdoor equipment shop used in this lab.

You can return any unused item within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with all tags attached.

Start a return from the Orders page of your account. We email you a prepaid return label. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the item reaches our warehouse.

Sale items marked "final sale" can't be returned. Opened camping stoves and fuel canisters can't be returned for safety reasons.
