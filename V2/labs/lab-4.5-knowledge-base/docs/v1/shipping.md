# Shipping

Trailhead Gear ships from one warehouse, and most orders leave within one business day.

- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over 75 dollars.
- Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 dollars.
- We don't ship fuel canisters by air, so orders that contain them always go by standard shipping.

You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse.
