# Expenses FAQ

Short answers to the questions the finance team hears most. The Travel and Expense Policy is the source of truth.

**Can I book a hotel myself if it's cheaper?**
Not usually. Bookings outside the travel portal are only reimbursed when the portal had no option.

**Is there a hotel budget for team offsites?**
Offsites are booked by the events team, who negotiate a group rate. You don't need to book or claim anything yourself.

**Do I get a per diem?**
The company doesn't pay a flat per diem. Meals are reimbursed against receipts, up to the daily meal allowance.

**What happens if I submit a claim late?**
Late claims are paid if they arrive within 60 days, but your manager is notified.

**Can I upgrade my flight with my own miles?**
Yes. Upgrades paid with your personal miles or points are fine, as long as the company paid only for the allowed fare.

**Who pays for travel insurance?**
Every employee on company business is covered by the company's travel insurance. Don't buy your own.
