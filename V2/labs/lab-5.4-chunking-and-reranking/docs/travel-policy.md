# Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3. Applies to all employees and contractors who travel for company business.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how to book business travel, what the company pays for, and how to claim it back. It aims to keep travel safe, fair and cost-effective. When this policy and a local rule disagree, the stricter rule applies.

## 2. Booking travel

Book all flights, trains and hotels through the company travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed unless the portal had no option for the route or the date. In that case, attach a screenshot of the portal search to your claim.

Book at least 14 days before you travel when you can. Late bookings cost more, and your manager will see every booking made less than 7 days before departure.

## 3. Flights

Economy class is the default for every flight.

Premium economy is allowed when the scheduled flight time is more than 6 hours.

Business class is allowed only when the scheduled flight time is more than 10 hours and the traveller is expected to work on arrival the same day. A director must approve it in the portal before you book. Without that approval, the company pays the economy fare and you pay the difference.

Checked bags are covered for trips longer than three nights.

## 4. Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 dollars, including taxes.

Some cities cost more. In London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich the nightly limit is 260 dollars.

Managers and above may exceed the city limit by up to 20 percent when the conference hotel is the only option within walking distance of the event. This exception must be noted in the claim.

Stays longer than 14 nights in one city must use serviced apartments booked through the portal.

## 5. Meals

The daily meal allowance is 75 dollars. It covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and non-alcoholic drinks.

If the hotel rate includes breakfast, the daily allowance drops to 60 dollars.

Alcohol is never reimbursed, except at a client dinner approved in advance by a director, and then for no more than two drinks per person.

## 6. Ground transport

Use public transport where it is safe and practical. Taxis and ride-hailing are allowed between 9 pm and 6 am, when travelling with heavy equipment, or when no public option takes less than twice as long.

Car rental needs manager approval and is limited to a compact car. Fuel is reimbursed at cost; mileage for a personal car is paid at the published company rate.

## 7. Claiming expenses

Submit claims in the expense tool within 30 days of returning. Claims submitted after 60 days are not paid.

Attach an itemised receipt for every expense over 25 dollars. For expenses of 25 dollars or less, a description is enough.

Lost receipts: you may submit one missing-receipt declaration per trip, for up to 100 dollars. Above that, the expense is not reimbursed.

## 8. Approvals and audits

Your manager approves every claim. Claims above 5,000 dollars also need finance approval.

Finance audits a random sample of claims each quarter. Claims that break this policy are returned, and repeated breaches are reported to the traveller's director.
