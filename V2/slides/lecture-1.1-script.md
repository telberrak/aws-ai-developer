> Delivery marks: **bold** = stress · / = brief pause · [pause] = 1 second · [long pause] = 2 to 3 seconds. ▶ CLICK = advance to the next build. "Transition:" is the spoken bridge into what comes next.

## 1. How this course and the exam fit together

(About 45 seconds)

Welcome, / and thank you for joining me. [pause]

I'm a solution architect, / and I've spent twenty years building technology in banking. / So I'll teach this the way I'd explain it to a new person on my team. / Plainly, / and with real systems in mind.

My goal is simple. / I want you to pass the AWS Certified Generative AI Developer Professional exam, / **AIP-C01**, / on your **first** attempt.

In this first lecture, / I'll cover three things. / First, / what the exam really tests. / Then, / how this course is built around it. / And finally, / a six-week plan you can follow. [pause]

If I had to sum it up in one sentence, / it would be this. [pause] The exam tests a **builder**, / and this course spends your time where the points are. / Keep that in mind, / because everything else hangs on it. [long pause]

Transition: So, / what does the exam really test?

## 2. A builder's exam: can you ship GenAI on AWS?

(About 1 min)

Here's the thing most people don't expect. / This exam isn't about theory. / It asks one question, / over and over: / can you **build** generative AI on AWS, / and run it well? [pause]

▶ CLICK

First, / building. / You'll design **RAG** solutions, / so a model can answer from your own documents. / You'll plug foundation models into real applications. / You'll write and manage prompts, / the same way you manage code. / And you'll build **agents** / that call tools and take actions. [pause]

▶ CLICK

Now, / running it. / That means keeping it fast and affordable. / Keeping it **secure**, / and using AI responsibly. / And when something goes wrong, / knowing how to monitor it, / fix it / and measure its quality. [pause]

▶ CLICK

And here's the good news. [pause] You **won't** be asked to train a model from scratch. / You use models, / and you customize them. / You don't build them. / So if you've built applications on AWS before, / you're closer than you think. [long pause]

Transition: So, / how does the exam turn all of that into questions?

## 3. Five domains, five weights

(About 1 min 10 s)

The exam is split into **five** areas. / AWS calls them domains, / and each one has a weight. / The weight is simply the share of questions that come from it. [pause]

▶ CLICK

Domain 1 is the biggest, / at **thirty-one** percent. / That's foundation models, / your data, / vector stores, / retrieval / and prompts. / In simple terms, / getting the right information to the model.

▶ CLICK

Domain 2 is **twenty-six** percent. / That's agents, / deployment, / and connecting GenAI to the systems a company already has.

▶ CLICK

Domain 3 is twenty percent. / Safety, / security / and governance. / In other words, / keeping data protected / and answers safe.

▶ CLICK

Domain 4 is twelve percent. / That's cost, / performance / and monitoring.

▶ CLICK

And Domain 5 is eleven percent. / Testing, / evaluation / and troubleshooting. [pause]

▶ CLICK

So, / Domains 1 and 2 together are **fifty-seven** percent. / That's more than half your score. [pause] But please don't skip the small ones. / Honestly, / a few easy questions from Domain 5 / can be the difference between a pass and a fail. [long pause]

Transition: And that's exactly how I've shaped this course.

## 6. Six weeks at about six hours a week

(About 1 min)

Here's a plan that works for most people. / **Six** weeks, / at about six hours a week. [pause] If you already build with Bedrock every day, / you can go faster. / If GenAI is new to you, / take eight weeks. / That's completely fine. [pause]

▶ CLICK

In week one, / you cover Sections 1 to 4. / Setup, / design, / choosing models / and data. / And you take the diagnostic test, / so you know your starting point.

▶ CLICK

Week two is retrieval and prompts. / Then you take the Domain 1 checkpoint. / Aim for **seventy-five** percent or more.

▶ CLICK

Week three is agents, / deployment / and APIs, / with the Domain 2 checkpoint at the end.

▶ CLICK

Week four is safety, / security / and governance.

▶ CLICK

Week five is cost, / monitoring, / evaluation / and troubleshooting.

▶ CLICK

And week six is all about practice. [pause] My advice: / book the real exam / when you score **eighty** percent or more / on **two** practice exams. / Not one lucky run. / Two. [long pause]
